## State License Applies in Fayetteville

North Carolina landscape contractor licensing is governed by Chapter 89D of the NC General Statutes and administered by the NCLCRB. The registration issued by the NCLCRB is valid statewide — there is no separate Fayetteville or Cumberland County landscape contractor license. Your NCLCRB registration is the credential that authorizes qualifying landscape contracting work throughout North Carolina, including in Fayetteville and the surrounding Sandhills region.

If you are performing activities in Fayetteville that fall within the scope of Chapter 89D — landscape installation, grading, irrigation, hardscape construction, and related work — a valid NCLCRB registration is required. The military community that makes up a significant portion of Fayetteville's population frequently requests professional, licensed contractors, so having your license in order is both a legal requirement and a practical business advantage.

## Requirements for Fayetteville Contractors

The requirements to obtain your NCLCRB landscape contractor license are the same statewide standards regardless of where in North Carolina you plan to work. You must meet experience requirements, pass the state landscape contractor examination, carry required liability insurance, and pay applicable fees. Your registration must be renewed on schedule to remain active.

The NCLCRB exam covers landscape installation practices, plant identification, grading and drainage, irrigation, and business and legal topics. Fayetteville-area contractors should contact the NCLCRB directly to identify the nearest approved exam testing location. Fayetteville Technical Community College offers programs in horticulture and related fields that can help candidates build the foundational knowledge tested on the state exam.

## Working in Cumberland County

Cumberland County sits at the transition between the Coastal Plain and the Sandhills region of North Carolina, giving Fayetteville a distinct landscape environment. The sandy, well-drained soils common throughout much of the area differ significantly from the clay soils found in the Piedmont — this affects plant selection, irrigation design, and turf establishment practices. Sandy soils drain quickly, which can mean more frequent irrigation needs for ornamental plantings but also presents excellent conditions for certain types of installation work.

Fayetteville's climate is warm, with mild winters and a long growing season, making it one of the earlier spring markets in inland North Carolina. The city's residential landscape market is substantial, fueled in large part by active-duty military families, veterans, and DoD civilian employees who live on and around Fort Liberty. Property turnaround, new homeowner landscape installations, and ongoing maintenance contracts are steady sources of work. Commercial landscape work around retail corridors, medical facilities, and the broader infrastructure supporting the military community adds to the opportunity.

## Local Permits and Regulations

Your NCLCRB registration is your state-level landscape contractor credential, but local permits in Fayetteville and Cumberland County may also be required for certain types of work. These are separate from your state license and must be obtained from local authorities.

The City of Fayetteville and Cumberland County may require permits for grading or land-disturbing activity above threshold acreages, retaining wall construction, and certain irrigation system installations. Projects that affect stormwater drainage or are located in flood-prone areas may face additional review. Fort Liberty itself is a federal installation with its own contractor access and permitting processes that apply to work performed on post — if you plan to work on base, contact the relevant contracting office at Fort Liberty for those requirements separately. For work within the city and county, contact the Fayetteville Inspections and Permits Division or Cumberland County Planning for current requirements.

## Getting Started in Fayetteville

To get your NC landscape contractor license for work in Fayetteville, begin by reviewing the NCLCRB's eligibility requirements, particularly the experience documentation standards. Contact the NCLCRB to obtain your application, confirm current exam dates and test site locations, and review the current fee schedule.
