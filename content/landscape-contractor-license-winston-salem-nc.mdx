## State License Applies in Winston-Salem

North Carolina landscape contractor licensing is governed by Chapter 89D of the NC General Statutes and administered by the NCLCRB. The state registration issued by the board is valid for landscape contracting work anywhere in North Carolina — there is no separate Winston-Salem city license or Forsyth County license for this profession.

If you are performing work in Winston-Salem that falls within the scope of Chapter 89D — including landscape installation, grading, irrigation systems, and certain hardscape work — you must hold a current NCLCRB registration. This requirement applies to contractors working in every part of the city, from new residential developments on the outskirts to renovation projects in established neighborhoods close to downtown.

## Requirements for Winston-Salem Contractors

The requirements for obtaining your NCLCRB landscape contractor registration are the same statewide standards that apply to contractors across North Carolina. You must document qualifying field experience, pass the state landscape contractor examination, maintain the required liability insurance coverage, and pay applicable registration and renewal fees.

The NCLCRB exam covers plant identification, landscape installation practices, grading and drainage fundamentals, irrigation, and business and legal knowledge. Winston-Salem's landscape market emphasizes work with mature trees, ornamental beds, and period-appropriate plantings in historic districts — this on-the-ground expertise strengthens your exam preparation even if the test itself covers broader statewide content. Forsyth Technical Community College offers horticulture-related courses that can help candidates prepare. Contact the NCLCRB for current exam site information serving the Piedmont Triad.

## Working in Forsyth County

Forsyth County encompasses Winston-Salem along with smaller communities including Kernersville, Lewisville, and Clemmons. The county offers a landscape market shaped by a stable, diverse economy rooted in healthcare, financial services, manufacturing, and higher education — Wake Forest University's campus is a prominent institutional presence, and Novant Health and Atrium Health both operate major facilities in the area.

Winston-Salem's four-season Piedmont climate is moderate and forgiving for landscape work. Summers are warm and humid, winters bring occasional ice and some snow, and both spring and fall offer ideal planting conditions. The city is distinguished by its wealth of historic neighborhoods — Ardmore, West End, Buena Vista, and Reynolda Road — where mature oak, elm, and beech canopies define the landscape character. Work in these areas often involves integrating new plantings and hardscapes sensitively around established trees, navigating root zones, and matching the scale and character of older landscapes. Commercial landscape work is active along major corridors and around the city's growing medical and university districts.

## Local Permits and Regulations

Your NCLCRB registration is your statewide credential for landscape contracting but does not replace permits required by the City of Winston-Salem or Forsyth County. Local permitting requirements apply to certain types of landscape and site work independently of your state license.

The City of Winston-Salem may require permits for land-disturbing activities above specified thresholds, retaining walls above certain heights, and irrigation system connections. The city has an active urban forestry program, and work that involves tree removal or impacts root zones of significant trees may require review or permits under local tree ordinances. Winston-Salem also has stormwater management requirements that can affect grading and drainage projects. For work in unincorporated Forsyth County, the county's planning and inspections offices have their own permit processes. Before starting any project, confirm requirements with Winston-Salem's Inspections Division or Forsyth County Planning.

## Getting Started in Winston-Salem

To pursue your NC landscape contractor license for work in Winston-Salem, start by reviewing the NCLCRB eligibility requirements, particularly around experience documentation. Contact the NCLCRB to request current application materials, review exam schedules, and confirm fee information before you apply.
